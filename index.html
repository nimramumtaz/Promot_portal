The challenge

Video and prompt requirements

Your video is checked against these points.

Follow the brief

Use the provided story, scene instructions and required visual elements, and use the supplied images appropriately.

Prompt with structure

Write clear, structured prompts and refine them to improve motion, continuity and visual consistency.

Create it live

Make the video during the competition. Previously completed videos cannot be submitted.

Submit a playable MP4

Length, aspect ratio and any extra conditions will be stated in the task.

AI is the core

Editing, audio and subtitles are allowed, but AI generation must form the core video content.

How you will be judged

Four things decide the result. The judges' evaluation decision is final.

Prompt engineering

How clear, structured and refined your prompts are.

Scenario accuracy

How closely the video follows the story and required elements.

Visual quality

Motion, continuity and consistency.

Creativity

What you add to the brief that others did not.

Fair play

Judges may inspect prompts and verify your work.

  • Do not copy another team's prompts or work.
  • Do not fabricate generation evidence.
  • Do not include unauthorized or harmful content.

Common questions

Quick answers before you register.

Can I enter alone?

Yes. Enter individually or with one partner. A team has at most two members.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

Free, Pro and Premium tools are allowed. You arrange your own accounts, credits and subscriptions.

Can I use ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude for prompts?

Yes. AI assistants and editing software are allowed, but AI generation must form the core of the video.

Can I submit a video I made earlier?

No. The video must be created during the 120 minutes.

What do I submit?

A playable MP4. Length, aspect ratio and other conditions are given in the task. Audio and subtitles are allowed.
